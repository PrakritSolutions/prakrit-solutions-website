## Major workflows
1. Visitor browses, optionally accepts analytics cookies (banner shown only if a GA ID is set).
2. Visitor submits contact form: name, email, project required; optional company, phone, services, budget (INR/USD ranges), timeline, message. UTM/referrer captured from sessionStorage and turned into a short "source" label.
3. Server: honeypot check, 5/hour/IP in-memory rate limit, validation, console log, Sheet append (if configured), Resend email to `hello@prakritsolutions.in` with reply-to the visitor, confirmation email to the visitor.
4. Owner replies from Gmail, updates the Status column in the Sheet (see `integrations/google-sheets/README.md`).
5. "Book a 30-minute call" links to Calendly; WhatsApp-only number for chat.
